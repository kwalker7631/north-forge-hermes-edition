# What this program is

Built the way the work is built: pieces first, then the face.
The engine is far enough along. What still has to earn the room is
the writing and the walk-in.

## The thing itself

North Forge is a portable technical-support agent. A technician talks
in ordinary English. The stick already knows the shop. It runs **in
cooperation with Hermes Agent**. Hermes is the engine and keeps its
name. North Forge is the coworker, the pack, the well.

The engine is proprietary and will be sold. It is not for sale this
week. This week it has to survive a live call.

## Facade

One chassis. Many faces.

Kyocera is the first face: faults, firmware, drivers, PaperCut, MyQ,
hotline voice. The same stick shape can wear Sharp, Ricoh, Xerox, or
another service line when that pack is funded and written. That is not
a theme switch. It is another well on the same engine.

Public face: Hermes + North Forge name. No private well.
Private face: the well, locksmith, drive class, isolated home on the
volume.

## How we work (so the next engineer does not fight you)

You assemble, then polish. You will circle back to an old thread.
That is the method, not a defect. What it requires of the repo:

- One current map, not twelve overlapping READMEs.
- Labels and class names live in `DRIVE_LABELS.txt` and `PRODUCT_DRIVES.md`.
- Demo and money talk live in `DEMO_AND_SALES.md`.
- Pull steps live in `HAND_HOLD_PULL.md`.
- Older pages that disagree are history. Do not keep editing them
  into a second truth.

Speed of typing is irrelevant. Speed of a clean stick in someone
else's hand is the metric.

## Professional bar

This will sit in front of a global manufacturer. The walk-in should
look like TSC work, not a weekend repo.

Must be true in the room:

- Two buttons on the stick: Terminal, Web.
- Gold-on-iron dashboard, caption: North Forge · in cooperation with
  Hermes Agent.
- One real code, answered like a tech, hedges when the well is empty.
- No Git, no PATH lecture, no Advanced folder.

If the documentation still feels thin, fund a single pass over the
*front* pages only (root README of each repo, HOW_TO_START, this file,
DEMO_AND_SALES). Do not fund a rewrite of the engine to make the
paper prettier.
