# Skills that may ride a stick

If it does not help a call, a van, or a repair, it does not ship.

## Keep

- Humanizer (always on TSC / Excalibur)
- Field operations: hotline, assist, check, KB, logs, escalation, firmware vs logs
- Record-keeping: fault log, ticket body, session notes that stay on the stick
- Expense / light financial: mileage, parts-out-of-pocket, simple tally — **only when a real skill exists**. Do not fake a general ledger.
- Navigation / mapping: get the tech to the site, store hours, dock directions — web maps via tools, not a games GPS.
- Repair of the device itself (knights): path check, pull, inference key

## Ban (never promote from hermes-extra)

Games, chess, truth or dare, party prompts, trivia, dating, generic "fun" agents, and any sales catalog that is not that person's job. Unrelated maker-lab tools (image toys, chess engines, roleplay).

`sales-assist` stays off Excalibur and BASIC unless the volume is a sales class. Pocket Penny is still its own face, not smuggled onto a TSE stick as "finance."

## Hermes extras

New upstream skills default **off**. A knight may enable one on BLACK-NORTH after you say it is field-useful. They may not enable chess because it shipped in an update.

## Phone

USB-C OTG on Android, and newer iPhones with a USB-C adapter, can see the stick as storage. That does not run `hermes.exe`. A relay app (phone talks to a small gateway on a PC or to Hermes messaging) is a later project. Do not promise iPhone Terminal this week.
