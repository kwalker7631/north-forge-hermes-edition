# Who holds which stick

## Advocate — BLACK-NORTH (you)

The admin stick exists because someone has to stand in the room and show
the thing. That is you. The zoo (Pinokio, Ollama, HF, Civitai, Docker,
voice demos) is **not preloaded**. When you have something to demonstrate,
you spin it up on this disk or on a lab PC. When the room empties, you
tear it down. Greg may use that lab only if he **brings a disk** and you
put it on. It is not his default volume.

## Knights — FIRSTL-NOREX (Excalibur)

Northridge technicians you are training to **run and repair** the device.
They are Excalibur. They get admin-class privileges on their stick so they
can fix a dead venv, paste a new 30-day key, pull the pack, spawn a BASIC
stick from the console when you say so. They do not get the imagination
lab. Their job is the desk plus keeping the desk alive.

First knight in production: **GREGW-NOREX**.

## Techs — FIRSTL-NORTH / BASIC-NORTH

One purpose: technical support. Talk to North Forge. `/hl` `/chk` `/clr`.
They do not explore AI, Discover apps, Docker, or theme hunting. They do
not open Advanced. If it breaks, they hand it to a knight — they do not
tinker.

## Tamper

If it is not on the allowlist for that class, it does not belong on that
volume. Knights repair North Forge. They do not turn a TSE stick into a
playground because they saw you demo Comfy.
