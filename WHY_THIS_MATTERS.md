# Why this is worth your week

If you already live in the Blue Book, you do not need another slogan.
You already know the work is supposed to be just, earnest, and for the
person in front of you. This page is only this: **why you should pick
the drive up and use it**, so the pack can learn from a real desk and
earn the right to stay.

A tool nobody uses is a hobby. Feedback is the glory. Silence is a
failed handoff.

## What we are asking you to do

1. Take the labeled drive.
2. On the next real call — not a demo call — start North Forge.
3. Talk the way you talk to the person you actually call.
4. When it is thin, say so. When it is wrong, correct it.
5. Hand the correction back. That is how the pack accumulates effort.

That is the sale. Not a feature matrix. A request to let the work
touch the floor.

## Why a person who reads the books every week should care

Search is a pile of pages. A generic assistant is a pile of pages with
manners. Neither one is bound to your fleet, your last tickets, or the
habit of saying “I do not have that page.”

This pack was written on the side of the job by someone who took the
founder's gift seriously: do what is right, put the customer first,
accumulate the effort that does not get a parade. The programming is
the tedious part. The README is not decoration. It is the bond — so
the next person is not starting from zero.

If the writing is flat, people will not use it.
If people will not use it, the pack cannot learn.
If the pack cannot learn, it does not deserve the desk.

Glory, here, is a manager who watches one call go cleaner — not a
star count on GitHub.

Use it, or tell us why you will not. Either one is respect.
